# M6: Excel and PDF

Started 2026-10-24 while Zoe was out; unverified on the device until she returns.

## Round 1: XLSX and PDF export (US-23, US-24; `src/export2.c`, `exp2` section)

- **XLSX**: the SP5 writer (STORED zip, seek-patched headers through the card OutStream, inline
  strings) with five sheets. Fills carries the CSV columns as typed cells: dates as Excel
  dates, numbers with 1, 2 or 4 decimals, totals in the fill's currency format (CAD or USD
  styles, else 2 dp), the home total in the home currency's. Monthly runs the report engine per
  year from the first to the last fill: year, month, fills, volume, spend, average price,
  economy, points. Vehicles, Programmes (with balances) and Services (visit rows with the
  services named) complete the workbook. Vehicle and year filters apply to Fills, Monthly and
  Services.
- **PDF**: the SP7 writer. Page 1 is the summary (fills, distance, fuel, spend, average, best
  and worst, average price, cost per 100 km, service spend); then log pages ruled like the
  paper form, 28 rows each (date, odometer, trip, volume, price, total with currency, points,
  notes) up to fourteen pages; then an economy graph of the fuel full-to-full segments with a
  dashed mean and the first and last dates.
- The Export form writes whichever format is chosen; the CRC table, the report and stats
  accumulators and the graph arrays live on the heap or the stack, because the static data
  segment (A5 world) filled up at 32 KB during this round ("region datares is full"). The
  graph arrays moved to the heap for the same reason.
