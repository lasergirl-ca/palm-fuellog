# M3: Stats, reports and service

Started 2026-10-23 after M2's forms were all in place (the odometer alert, the USD rate field
and a real redemption are left to check on the road). Zoe wants the app usable for a real
test drive, so Stats comes first, then Service.

## Stats (US-13, US-15 and part of US-16; build 1023-0640)

- `src/stats.c` (xport, plain C, host-tested by `tests/test_stats.c`): a running accumulator
  fed fills oldest first. Distance is last minus first odometer per vehicle, summed. Economy
  is full tank to full tank per vehicle, with separate fuel and charge chains, a missed fill
  breaking the chain, partial fills rolling into the next full one, and the fleet figure
  pooling the full-to-full totals rather than averaging cars. It also keeps best and worst
  segments with dates, low and high price with dates, spend by payment and by currency, and
  points earned, redeemed and saved per programme.
- The host test is the mock-ups' eight-fill Outback log: 8.3 L/100 km, $600.08, 3,562 km,
  339.9 L, best 8.1 (Oct 8) and worst 8.5, average price 176.5 c/L, low 174.9 and high 179.0,
  average fill 42.5 L, 11.7 days between fills. Cost per 100 km is $16.85 by the spec's
  formula (spend over distance); the mock-up's $14.62 was sample text, not the formula.
- `src/stats_form.c` (forms section): "Since Jul 29, 8 fills", the average economy and the
  spend in large bold type with captions, then label-value rows (Distance, Fuel used,
  Electricity or Energy used, Best / worst, Average price, Low / high price, Cost per 100 km,
  Average fill, Days between fills, Spend per month, Cash / card, Saved by points), then
  Points with each programme's balance and "about $". The vehicle popup in the title bar
  changes the current vehicle like the list's. Page keys scroll when the input area is open.
  An electric car gets kWh/100 km, kWh and c/kWh in place of the fuel figures. Reports and
  Graphs say "Not yet".

## Round 14 (build 1023-0700): push groups, gallons at the pump, rows under the buttons

- **Push groups never saved** (Zoe: "its not saving cents/L"). `FrmGetControlGroupSelection`
  returns the selected button's object index, while `FrmSetControlGroupSelection` takes its
  ID; every form compared the index to an ID, so the first option always won: price format
  stayed dollars, odometer unit km, payment Cash, programmes Points. `AppGroupSelection`
  returns the ID and all five forms use it. (Earlier device rounds never caught it because
  the first option was the one in use.)
- **Gallons at the pump** (Zoe): the fill form's Volume row has a unit popup (L, US gal,
  Imp gal) that sets the typing unit for volume and price together; the price label follows
  ("Price (c/gal)"). It starts at the Prefs unit, except a USD fill on a litres setup starts
  in US gallons, and it switches when the currency popup changes. Typed values convert on a
  switch; storage stays mL and per litre. Hidden for a charge.
- **Rows under the buttons**: with the input area open, the fill form's Points row and the
  lower Edit Programme rows landed in the band the pinned buttons occupy and drew over them.
  `AppShowByPosition` hides the listed rows while they sit under the title or the buttons and
  shows them again when a page key brings them into the scrolling area.
- Zoe asked what "Missed" meant: it marks a fill you did not log before this one, so the
  economy chain breaks here instead of crediting this tank with the unlogged distance. The
  checkbox is now "Skipped last fill" on its own row under Full tank (build 1023-0710), and
  the charge label is "Charged to full" again. The Points row moved down a row with it.

## Service (US-33 to US-35, first slice; build 1023-0730)

- `src/service.c` (xport, plain C, `tests/test_service.c`): due logic from the spec. km left is
  last odometer plus interval minus now; days left from the months; the distance limit becomes
  a projected date at the pace (km/day over the last 90 days of fills, at least 3, else over
  all fills); status is Overdue when either limit has passed, Soon within the warn window or
  when the projected date is within the warn days, else OK; "set up" when no interval is set
  and "no visit" when none is logged. The host test is the spec's Outback example: 640 km
  left, time due Nov 14, projected Nov 6 at 43 km/day, Soon; wipers 12 months from Oct 15
  2025 are 7 days overdue on Oct 22. Presets by powertrain: nine for fuel and hybrids, five
  for electric (no oil, plugs or transmission fluid); intervals blank, warn 14 days and 10 %
  of the distance once set.
- `src/service_form.c` (new `svcs` code section): the **Service** list (header "Odometer
  108,657 km, pace 43 km/day", rows by urgency with name, due date or "about" projected date,
  status word, and a second line saying why; footer "Last visit ..."; vehicle popup; Log, New,
  Done; page keys scroll). Presets install the first time a vehicle's list opens. Tapping a
  row opens **Service Type** (name, every N km, or every N months, warn km or days, alarm
  flag, default detail, and a three-line "with these settings" summary; Delete when no visit
  covers it, Archive when one does, Restore when archived). **Log Service**: date, odometer
  from the last fill, a checklist of the vehicle's services plus Other with a few words, cost,
  currency, Cash/Card, shop, detail, Note; a "Next Oil and filter: 116,657 km or Apr 23" line
  appears as items are ticked. Visits save sorted by date.
- Fill list: a banner line under the title ("Oil and filter due in 640 km", "Wiper blades
  overdue") and the Service button reads "Service!" while anything is Soon or Overdue for the
  vehicle shown (All vehicles scans every live car).
- Not yet: the alarm (`AlmSetAlarm` and the reset relaunch), the alert when a fill tips a
  service into Soon, service History with gaps and running cost (US-36), editing or deleting
  a visit, promoting an Other item to a type.

## Overnight round A (build 1023-0900): History, visit editing, running cost

- **History** (tap a service row that has a visit, or the History button for every visit):
  name and interval line, headlines "longest gap", "average" and the spend over N visits,
  then one row per visit newest first: date (with year when not this year), odometer, cost,
  and under it the gap since the visit before with months, in bold with "over distance",
  "over time" or "over both" when it ran past the interval. "first record" closes the list.
  Intervals opens the Service Type form; tapping a visit opens it for editing.
- **Edit Visit**: Log Service loads an existing visit (title "Edit Visit"), with Delete and a
  confirm; saving re-sorts by date. Rows with no visit yet still open the Service Type form.
- **Stats**: "Service spend" and "With service /100 km" rows when the vehicle has visits.
- 5-way pad: up and down step lists and forms one row; page keys move a screenful.

## Overnight round B (build 1023-0920): alarm and the due alert (US-35)

- `SvcScheduleAlarm` sets one system alarm (`AlmSetAlarm`, 9:00 on the earliest due date among
  services with "Alarm when due", across live vehicles) and remembers the time in Prefs
  (`nextAlarm`, prefs v2; a v1 blob upgrades in place). It runs at launch and after any fill,
  visit or service type change. On `sysAppLaunchCmdSystemReset` the app re-arms it from Prefs
  without touching globals or other code sections; `sysAppLaunchCmdDisplayAlarm` shows
  "FuelLog: service due".
- Saving a fill that tips a service into Soon or Overdue shows one alert with the banner text.
