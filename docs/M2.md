# M2: Entry UI

**Status (Oct 22, 2026): started.** The fill list and the fill form are built over the real
databases (US-04, US-05 and the trip check of US-06); the device check is pending. The exit
criterion is a fill logged in under 20 seconds, with vehicles, programmes, currencies,
payment and charging all editable on the device.

## Done so far

- **Fill list** (`src/fill_list.c`): FuelLog's home base. One row per fill, newest first, drawn
  by hand so numbers sit on the decimal: date, odometer, volume, total, and economy in bold
  (full tank to full tank; "partial" otherwise). Striped rows from the Colour Theme. A footer
  with the lifetime average and this month's spend in home currency. Tap a row to edit it,
  New to add, page keys (the 5-way up and down on the T|X) to scroll. The empty state says
  what to tap. Stats and Service say "not yet". The form follows the input area.
- **Fill form** (`src/fill_form.c`): date and time selectors (both pre-filled from the clock; the
  time is a new 16-bit field in the fill record, minutes since midnight), odometer with a "p: 84,212" hint (the previous odometer), trip
  with a live check ("= odo" or "check odo", at 2 % or 5 km whichever is larger, and
  "odo 11,311" when the odometer is blank), volume, price and total where any two give the
  third and the computed one is marked "calculated", currency popup, Cash/Card push
  buttons, grade popup, Full tank. Labels carry the units from Prefs, prices follow the
  dollars-or-cents setting, and separators follow the Formats panel. Done validates and
  saves in sort order; editing replaces the record; Delete asks first. The last payment
  method and currency become the defaults for the next fill.
- **Helpers**: `src/text.c` parses and formats fixed-point numbers; `src/datecalc.c` does
  the 1904-based calendar (the record format never touches Palm's 7-bit year); `src/app.c`
  holds Prefs, separators, field helpers and the input-area setup every form shares.
- **Diagnostics** moved behind Options > Diagnostics, with the spike buttons as before.

## Decisions on the device

- The fill form's labels are in the standard font, not bold as the Look and feel table says:
  at 160 pixels the bold font's "Odometer:" alone takes a third of the width and left no
  room for the "prev" hint. Titles and list headers stay bold.

## What to check on the T|X

1. Launch. The list should be empty with "No fills yet. Tap New to log one." and the buttons
   New, Stats, Service along the bottom.
2. Tap New. Type an odometer, a volume and a price (in cents per litre, the default); Total
   should fill itself and say "calculated". Tap Done. The row should appear with the date,
   odometer, volume, total and "partial" or no economy (there is no earlier full fill yet).
3. Add a second fill with a higher odometer and Full tank checked. Its row should now show
   an economy in bold, and the footer an average.
4. Type a trip that matches the odometer change: the hint should read "matches odo". Type one
   that does not: "gap: check odo". Clear the odometer and leave the trip: "odo = …".
5. Tap a row, change the volume, tap Done: the row updates. Tap a row, Delete, confirm: it
   goes. Collapse the input area on either form: everything refits.
6. Time a fill from New to the row appearing. The target is under 20 seconds.

## Device result

First run: a white screen at launch with no title bar and no status bar, different from any
earlier failure. Two changes before the second run, since the cause could not be found by
reading: the 68k stack is now 16 KB (`stack=16384` in `fuellog.def`; prc-tools requests 4 KB
by default, and the draw path carried several 330-byte fill records plus Palm's own frames),
the large record buffers in the list, the form and `db.c` are static rather than on the stack,
and `PilotMain` drew a breadcrumb word after each launch stage.

Second and third runs: every launch stage completed, and finer breadcrumbs showed the form
loading, initialising and activating, then nothing. The disassembly explained it: the form-load
code in `main.c` registered handlers only for the diagnostics and results forms. An edit that
should have added the fill list and fill form registrations had silently failed to match after
an earlier rename, so the list form opened with no handler, never drew, and the app sat idle in
its event loop. Not a hang at all. The registrations are now a switch over the form IDs, the
breadcrumbs are gone, and the larger stack stays as a safety margin. Lesson for the Makefile
of the mind: an edit script that replaces text must fail loudly when the text is not found,
which every later edit here now does.

Later runs, Oct 22 evening, on the T|X:

- The empty list, the Options menu and the About alert all draw; the list refits to the
  collapsed input area.
- The fill form opened with a blank date selector and a label running into its field. Both
  mine: `CtlSetLabel` keeps a pointer, so the date text now lives in a static buffer; the
  labels moved to the standard font and the fields left. A time-of-day field was added to
  the record and the form at Zoe's request, pre-filled from the clock.
- The live total did not update while typing: Palm's `fldChangedEvent` never reached the
  form. The form now recomputes after each keystroke itself. With that, 51 L at 2.09 ¢/L
  showed $1.07 and "calculated" as typed, and a 200 km trip against a 901 km odometer move
  showed the mismatch hint.
- Two fills saved. The list shows the newer first, 51.00 L and $106.59 with **5.7** L/100 km
  in bold (51 L over 901 km), the older with no economy, and the footer "Avg 5.7 L/100 km"
  and "Oct $122.83", all of which check by hand.

- Edit: the older fill reopened, its price changed to 201 ¢/L, and the row showed $112.56 (56 L)
  with the footer at $219.15; the record kept its place in the sort.

- Delete: the older fill went after the confirm; the remaining row lost its economy figure,
  since nothing precedes it now, and the footer dropped to $106.59 with no average. US-05 done.

- The "Delete this fill?" confirmation shows over the dimmed edit form with Delete and Cancel;
  Cancel is now the default button. The edit form reloads every stored value; its title now
  reads "Edit Fill", and the odometer shows whole units.
