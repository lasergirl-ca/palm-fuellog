# M4: Graphs

Started overnight on 2026-10-23 after M3's Reports. First cut in build 1023-0950.

## Graphs (US-17, US-18; `src/graphs_form.c`, `rpts` section)

Stats > Graphs, or Reports > Graphs. A popup picks Economy (one point per full-to-full
segment, in the Prefs unit; kWh/100 km for a charge chain), Price per fill (fuel fills with a
price, in the Prefs price unit and format) or Spend per month (home currency, every month from
the first fill). The plot draws at double density: grey axes, a dashed mean line, a line with
dots per series, the low and high values and the bold mean at the left, month names along the
bottom (thinned so they do not collide), and a summary line "Low 8.1, high 8.5, mean 8.3".
Under All vehicles each car gets its own colour with a legend above the plot. Tapping within
20 px of a point names it ("Oct 08: 8.1") in a callout; tapping again clears it. Up to 256
points; the vehicle popup in the title bar changes the current vehicle like the other screens.

Not yet: min/max/mean labels on the axis ticks themselves, a per-vehicle legend beyond eight
cars, and the 500-fill timing check from the milestone definition.

## Economy unit on the graph (2026-11-10)

The economy graph plotted in the Prefs economy unit without saying which, and the fill list
shows km/L when Prefs is L/100 km, so the two disagreed with nothing to explain it. The
Graphs form now has a unit popup at the top right when Economy is selected: L/100 km, km/L,
MPG (US) or MPG (Imp). It shows and sets the Prefs economy unit, so Stats and the list's
MPG/KPL column follow; there is one economy unit, changeable from two places. It is hidden
for Price per fill and Spend per month. With one electric car selected the popup gives way
to a plain "kWh/100 km" label, since charging has no unit choice yet.

Check on the T|X: Stats > Graphs, Economy: the popup names the unit; pick km/L and the
line, the Low/high/mean figures and the tap callout change; Done, and Stats shows km/L too.
